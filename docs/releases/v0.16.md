# v0.16 - unit commands and first-person presentation

Prepared locally as development Test102. Publication and live acceptance are pending.

Command your unit without leaving first person:

- **Q: Move / Attack.** Point the crosshair at ground, an enemy or a supported destructible target. Tap to walk, double-tap to run/charge. Hold and drag your aim left/right for the game's normal formation placement, then release.
- **E: Stop.** Halt the unit.
- **Z: Hold Here.** Tap to walk into formation at your captured position and facing. Double-tap to run to the same position. Native guard is enabled.
- **C: Follow Me.** Form beside/around you. Run when behind; once caught up, match your native walking/running pace. Looking around while stationary does not keep restarting orders.
- **R: Firing policy.** Cycle Hold Fire, Fire at Will and Fire on Command; a successful personal shot authorizes the command volley.

First-person presentation uses a closer owned near plane and eye-relative infantry weapon/arm look, preserving the neck/collar and native head/shoulder camera caches. Normal native death view and exact camera/pose restoration remain.

The existing possession, personal weapons/ammunition/reload, native effective accuracy, firearm spread reticles, descending projectile dashes, timed melee QTEs, independent AI, movement/fatigue, HUD, saved RTS return and siege traversal are retained. Mounted-bow visuals and the unresolved extended-play native effects crash remain known issues. See the full README and known issues for coverage limits.

Close SHOGUN 2 before installing and start a fresh normal Steam process afterward. The player setup does not install diagnostic recording. The published v0.15 downloads remain unchanged.
