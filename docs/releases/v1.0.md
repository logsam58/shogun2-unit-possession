# v1.0 — Shogun 2 First-Person Mod

Command the army, then join the fight. Select a unit, press F9 and take control of one of its soldiers. Fight in first or third person, use their weapons and abilities, and give orders without leaving the action. F10 returns you to the RTS camera.

## Download and play

Download **Shogun2-First-Person-v1.0-Setup.exe** from this release's Assets. Close Shogun 2, run setup, choose your original Windows Steam game folder, then launch through Steam. In a single-player land battle, select a unit and press F9. The installer is unsigned. The repository remains private until the owner opens access.

Setup reads artwork, unit cards, ability/status icons, fonts and the cropped “Shameful display!” voice from your own game. None of those resources are bundled in the download. The supported English local_en.pack is required; Windows N needs the Media Feature Pack. No separate Python, compiler or media tools are needed to play. Use Windows Installed apps to uninstall with the game closed.

## What you can do

- Play as ordinary soldiers, generals, officers, flag bearers and musicians with their own equipment.
- Use bows with variable draw strength, guns and thrown weapons, or fight through timed melee encounters using native animations.
- Ride, attempt native charges, mount/dismount and climb castle walls where supported.
- Switch to third person with P, scroll to adjust distance and hold middle mouse to orbit.
- Give move/attack and formation orders with Q, Stop with E, Hold Here with Z, Follow Me with C and fire orders with R.
- Toggle native melee mode with 1, cycle abilities/special ammunition with T and use them with G. Aim at friendlies for targeted abilities.
- Hide both HUDs with K while keeping relevant melee prompts visible.

See the [full controls and feature guide](../../README.md#controls) for mouse, movement, weapons and siege controls.

## Known bugs

**Charging and castle wall climbing sometimes work and sometimes do not.** Both remain unfinished. Battle crashes are unresolved. Ultra unit sizes with several armies can cause severe slowdown. Some first-person weapons/arms clip, mounted bows have presentation issues, and isolated enemy acquisition is incomplete. Bow draw audio follows the original animation. See [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
