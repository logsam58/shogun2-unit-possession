## Native melee cleanup

The owner reproduced a late-battle siege failure in revision38: a lone live
defender was ignored while attackers approached and turned away. A read-only
check found an orphaned combat reservation with no active encounter. The QTE
had kept two native-selected fighters from a larger candidate list but failed
to release the excluded candidates' native busy reservations. Those soldiers
could then remain ineligible for melee after the encounter was destroyed.

Revision39 releases excluded reservations through original native cleanup after
successful pair retention, while keeping both selected fighters reserved. The
original target selection and combat outcome rules remain. Revision38's separate
current-possession reservation guard is retained. See
[candidate cleanup](../development/MeleeCandidates213.md) and
[reservation lifetime](../development/MeleeReservation212.md).

## Loader coexistence

Revision37 corrects the Deck UI contract around an unrelated renderer callback
used by Faster Load Times / Shogun2 Loader 0.5.2. All other UI code and table
entries remain guarded. Unit Possession does not call, replace or chain the
other mod's callback, and the external loader is not included or required.
See [renderer coexistence](../development/RendererCoexistence211.md).

## Verification and limits

A fresh full build passed 100 adapter, 51 QTE and 8 HUD executables, plus startup,
float-Lua, resource, menu and campaign checks. Original-engine fixtures passed
60 candidate-cleanup and 16 possession-reservation cases on the owned stock and
reviewed Bugfix engines. Renderer checks covered 880 refused mutations and 4,896
original projection/inverse points. Seven package-reader cases and exact PC/Deck
rollback checks passed.

Revision39 was installed and hash-verified on PC and Deck. The owner reported
“success.” That report is manual acceptance; the exact battle coverage was not
specified, and a formal revision39 loaded-hash/hook gate was not completed.
No exhaustive siege, roster, mod-combination or long-session guarantee follows.

The accepted Deck controls, charge improvements, K cycle, smoother look,
third-person framing, infantry/cavalry locomotion and revision36 Start Game
footer correction are retained. Large campaign panels still use the smaller
fit workaround. Campaign/battle controller failures, intermittent climbing,
crashes and isolated-player enemy acquisition remain open.
