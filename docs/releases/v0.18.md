## Move, fight and look around

Use **WASD** to move, **Shift** to run and the mouse to look. Movement follows the unit's native parameters. Personal movement is locked while knocked down and until the native get-up animation finishes; knockback, root motion and the recovery animation continue normally.

Press **1 / 2** for melee/ranged equipment. Hold **RMB** to ready a supported ranged weapon and press **LMB** to fire/release. **Tab** cycles available native ammunition. Hold **Ctrl** for crouched firing on supported, unlocked firearm sets. Native ammunition, reload, projectile speed, gravity and collision are retained.
