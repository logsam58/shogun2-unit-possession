# v0.38 — Unit Possession source

Revision 142 matches the November 15, 2026 update to the existing
[Steam Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3825625050).

- Last Alliance 0.5.2 loads with its original nine packs. The reader allows
  unrelated duplicate texture paths and keeps its validation for unsafe paths,
  invalid offsets, duplicate UI roots and duplicate Unit Possession metadata.
- Improved modded portraits and startup recovery. Players can keep their
  installed game language. HUD text is English.
- Right Alt opens Commander on keyboard. Key 3 pauses and resumes; key 4
  switches between slow motion and normal speed.
- During possession on Deck, R4 cycles slow motion, pause and normal speed.
  Hold R3 to follow an arrow while aiming a bow or during its flight. The
  next hold without an eligible arrow switches perspective.
- Deck controls support formation-preserving drag and two-trackpad adjustment
  of the selected group’s frontage in place.

The [validation record](../public-release/V0_38_VALIDATION.json) records the
Workshop delivery, offline checks and earlier gameplay reports. The source
tag distributes source only. The Testing branch retains its naval and Steam
Remote Play experiments.
