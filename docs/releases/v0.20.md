# v0.20 — Shogun 2 First-Person Possession

Jump into your Shogun 2 battles as one of your soldiers. Move with **WASD**, aim and fire your weapon, fight in melee, give your unit orders and climb siege walls. **F10** takes you back to commanding the army from your previous camera view.

The rest of your unit keeps fighting while you're in first person. Bows, guns, ammunition changes, accuracy-based spread, cavalry, death views and a small HUD are all part of the mod. The [README](../../README.md) has the full guide.

## What's new

- A cleaner HUD, with unit commands down the right side and weapon information kept out of the way until it's needed.
- A compact melee meter at the top of the screen, with bigger mouse-button prompts.
- Tighter QTE click handling and less repeated work during crowded melee. Big fights can still lag; that problem is still being investigated.
- The original **“SHAMEFUL DISPLAY!”** callout when you accidentally shoot an ally, without the sentence that normally comes before it.

## Unit orders

| Key | Order |
| --- | --- |
| **Q** | Point at the ground to move, or an enemy or supported breakable target to attack. Tap to walk; double-tap to run or charge. Hold Q and drag left to right to set the formation, then release. |
| **E** | Stop. |
| **Z** | Hold the position and facing you had when you pressed it. Tap to walk there; double-tap to run. |
| **C** | Follow you and form up beside you. Run to catch up, then match your pace. |
| **R** | Cycle Hold Fire, Fire at Will and Fire on Command. In the last mode, the unit fires when you fire. |

Q uses the game's usual formation placement. Your unit still has to deal with terrain, fatigue and morale.

## The other controls

- **F9** to possess; **F10 / Escape** to return to the RTS view.
- **WASD** to move, **Shift** to run and the **mouse** to look around, including during melee animations.
- Hold **RMB** to ready your ranged weapon; **LMB** to fire. **1 / 2** chooses melee/ranged equipment, **Tab** changes available ammo, and **Ctrl** gives crouched firing where supported.
- In melee, click the shown **LMB / RMB** when the marker reaches the highlighted zone. You get at least three in-game seconds and one full cycle unless the fight interrupts the prompt. Pausing pauses the meter.
- At a climbable wall, use the **F** prompt, then **W / S** to climb up/down. Release both to hold. Your soldier climbs over at the top. You can also possess a soldier who's already climbing.
- **F12** turns the mod off for the session.

## Install

Download **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.20-Setup.exe** from the assets below. Close Shogun 2 completely, run setup, then launch through Steam. In a land battle, select a unit and press F9. The HUD and combat prompts start automatically.

The Windows ZIP contains the installer and documentation. The source ZIP is for contributors. Checksum files are also included.

## Still being worked on

Crashes and occasional severe slowdowns in crowded fights are still open problems. Mounted-bow visuals and some weapon/arm clipping also need work. Not every weapon, ammo type or castle layout has been tested. This release is for single-player land battles and sieges; multiplayer and overhaul mods aren't confirmed to work.

Read the [known problems](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) if something goes wrong. Build details, test results and the crash investigation are in the [technical notes](v0.20-technical.md).
