# v0.15 - Shogun 2 First-Person Possession

**Step out of the RTS camera and fight as one of your soldiers.** Possess a living member of a selected unit in Total War: SHOGUN 2, move and aim in first person, use their native weapons, fight timed melee encounters and climb siege walls while the armies and the rest of your unit continue the battle.

## Features

- **Possession and camera:** F9 takes control of an eligible soldier; F10 / Escape restores the RTS camera saved before entry. Native animated head/body tracking and a death-view sequence keep the view tied to the soldier.
- **Native movement:** walking, running, turning, terrain interaction and fatigue use native unit behavior. Cavalry keeps rider/horse ownership and separates steering from bounded mouse look.
- **Personal weapons:** recognized bows, firearms and thrown/projectile families use native ammunition, animations, reload and projectile physics. Select melee/ranged with 1 / 2, cycle available ammo with Tab and hold Ctrl for supported unlocked crouched fire.
- **Accuracy and aiming:** native effective unit accuracy, including current modifiers, drives a circular muzzle cone. Firearm crosshair spacing reflects spread; non-firearm projectiles use descending drop-reference dashes. Supported infantry weapon lowering/reloading preserves vertical aim.
- **Melee QTEs:** native combat pairing, clear LEFT CLICK / RIGHT CLICK prompts, at least three active-simulation seconds and one full indicator cycle before unanswered timeout, timestamped visual/input matching, and INTERRUPTED feedback for canceled events.
- **Siege traversal:** F starts a climb near an eligible wall, W ascends, S descends and releasing both holds. Native speed/animation, crest playback and landing preserve the first-person traversal.
- **Independent unit AI and orders:** other soldiers keep fighting and firing. F5 / F6 / F7 issue halt / advance / toggle-follow unit orders.
- **Contextual HUD:** native-style artwork, trim and fonts display unit strength/morale/fatigue, personal equipment/ammunition, reticles and relevant action prompts.
- **Integrated installation:** one setup installs the embedded runtime and selects the mod; recognized predecessor upgrades keep a rollback snapshot, interrupted installs restore changes, and unrelated injection files/caches are guarded.
- **Developer tools:** maintained C++ source, offline native/renderer/installer regression fixtures, build scripts and optional automatic crash-diagnostic source.

## Download and play

Download **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.15-Setup.exe**, or extract **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.15-Windows.zip** and run setup inside. Close the game first, choose its folder in the wizard, then launch normally through Steam. No separate companion launcher is needed. Enter a single-player land battle, select a living unit and press F9. Hold RMB to ready/draw and press LMB to fire/release; follow the displayed mouse-button instruction in melee. F12 disables the mod for the session.

The supported retail Empire.Retail.dll SHA256 is `022fae2e2da92b59b8b1b8ee52887003c624e3c8eca060b209f5d792a7f874f5`. Setup verifies compatibility. Attached SHA256SUMS.txt and release-manifest.json pin downloads and components. [Full controls and guide](https://github.com/logsam58/shogun2-first-person/blob/v0.15/README.md).

## Known issues

**An unresolved crash has occurred during extended siege play.** Captured dumps show a native effects-table dereference; the preceding state change and possible mod involvement remain unresolved. This release does not fix that crash.

Mounted-bow presentation remains problematic. Specialty units/ammunition, all siege maps, expansion rosters, multiplayer and overhaul compatibility are not fully verified. Naval possession/ship commands and crew possession are not implemented; artillery retains stock first-person behavior. Accuracy uses a circular conversion rather than exact stock rectangular scatter, descending dashes are not calibrated range marks, and QTE timing is software submission matching rather than physical scanout measurement. [Known issues and troubleshooting](https://github.com/logsam58/shogun2-first-person/blob/v0.15/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
