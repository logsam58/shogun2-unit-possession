# v0.28 - Shogun 2 First-Person Possession

You can play in third person now. Press **P** to switch, use the **mouse wheel** to set your distance, and hold the **middle mouse button** to look around your soldier. You keep moving in the same direction while orbiting, and your view stays put when a new melee encounter begins.

Want a particular soldier? Zoom all the way in with the RTS camera, point the mouse cursor at someone in your selected unit, and press **F9**. Generals, officers, flag bearers and musicians can be possessed too. They keep their own weapons and equipment. F9 from high above still picks a soldier for you.

**M** uses the unit's normal mount/dismount order, with the camera following your soldier through the animation. Wall climbing now uses the game's own approach and mounting path. **K** keeps the RTS and possession HUDs hidden or visible together; melee prompts remain visible.

The camera also stays in third person through death. Broken or shattered morale keeps you with your soldier until death; routing encounters use the game's outcome without a timing prompt.

Bow strength, unit abilities, personal charges, unit controls and the “Shameful display!” callout are all included. Crashes remain under investigation.

## Install

Close Shogun 2, run **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.28-Setup.exe**, choose the game folder, then launch through Steam. The Windows ZIP includes setup and player documentation. Contributors can use the source ZIP.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **F9** | Possess a soldier in the selected unit; at closest RTS zoom, aim the mouse cursor at who you want |
| **F10 / Escape** | Return to your previous RTS camera view |
| **WASD** | Move |
| **Shift** | Run; hold W + Shift toward an enemy to charge |
| **Mouse** | Look around |
| **P** | Switch first/third person |
| **Mouse wheel** in third person | Change camera distance |
| **Hold middle mouse** in third person | Orbit your soldier without changing movement direction |
| **M** | Mount/dismount the unit, where supported |
| **Hold RMB** | Ready or draw your ranged weapon |
| **LMB** | Fire guns/thrown weapons; hold and release for bow strength |
| **1** | Toggle the unit between native melee and ranged mode, when supported |
| **T** | Cycle the unit’s available abilities and special ammunition |
| **G** | Use or end the selected ability; aim at a friendly unit for Inspire |
| **Hold Ctrl** | Crouch to fire, where supported |
| **LMB / RMB prompt** | Click the shown button when the marker reaches the highlighted zone |
| **F** near a wall | Start climbing |
| **W / S** while climbing | Climb up / down; release both to hold position |
| **K** | Hide/show the HUD in RTS and possession; melee prompts remain visible |
| **F12** | Turn the mod off for the rest of the session |

### Unit orders

| Key | Order |
| --- | --- |
| **Q — Move / Attack** | Aim at the ground to move, or at an enemy or supported breakable target to attack. Tap to walk; double-tap to run or charge. Hold Q and drag left to right to set the formation, then release. |
| **E — Stop** | Stop the unit. |
| **Z — Hold Here** | Form up where you're standing, facing the direction you're looking when you press Z. Tap to walk there; double-tap to run. |
| **C — Follow Me** | Form up beside you. Run to catch up if left behind, then match your pace. When you stop, the unit finishes forming up around you. |
| **R — Fire orders** | Cycle **Hold Fire → Fire at Will → Fire on Command**. Fire on Command tells the unit to fire when you fire. |

You can't issue these orders while locked in a melee animation, climbing or landing.

## Still being worked on

Crashes, first-person weapon/arm clipping, mounted-bow presentation, enemies targeting you away from your unit, and Ultra-sized crowded battles remain open. Bow draw sound still follows the native animation. Not every unit, castle layout or weapon pairing has been tested. Multiplayer and overhaul mods need separate testing.
