# v0.19 - uncluttered HUD and friendly-fire voice

Shogun 2 First-Person Possession lets you fight as an existing soldier inside a normal Total War: SHOGUN 2 land battle. Select a living unit and press **F9** to enter. Your armies, other soldiers, formations, pathfinding, morale, fatigue and battle objectives continue under the native game simulation.

v0.19 retains the exact Test109 adapter, HUD and QTE accepted by the player. Release metadata, the integrated loader and setup package receive the new version. This package is prepared locally; GitHub publication is separate.

## New in v0.19

- **Less cluttered HUD:** the soldier card stays bottom left, Q/E/Z/C/R form a vertical legend at the right edge, and bottom-centre weapon/ammo/climbing information appears only when relevant. Original artwork, fonts and trim are retained.
- **Compact QTE:** the timing meter moves to the top centre, with a larger mouse-button graphic and no routine text. INTERRUPTED feedback remains. The accepted current-clock timing, pause, full-cycle minimum and presented-image input handling are unchanged.
- **Personal friendly-fire feedback:** a verified direct hit by your own tracked projectile on a living ally triggers the original “SHAMEFUL DISPLAY!” voice. The preceding “Our men are running from the battlefield” sentence is removed. Playback is asynchronous in the separate HUD process; overlapping clips are dropped rather than queued. Enemy/AI/stale-projectile hits are rejected. Area-only explosions, secondary effects and every weapon/device combination are not certified.

Native fullscreen DirectX 9/11 presentation, independent AI/firing, movement, accuracy, unit commands, recovery locking, combat head-look, saved RTS camera return and siege traversal are retained.

## Unit controls in first person

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Q** | Aim at ground, an enemy or a supported destructible target. Tap for the native walk/attack order; double-tap for run/charge. Hold and drag your aim left/right to place the game's normal formation, then release. |
| **E** | Stop the unit. |
| **Z** | Hold Here at the position and facing captured when pressed, with native guard enabled. Single-tap to walk; double-tap to run to the same anchor. |
| **C** | Follow Me, forming beside/around the possessed soldier. Run to catch up, then match native walking/running pace. Looking around while stationary does not keep restarting the order. |
| **R** | Cycle Hold Fire, Fire at Will and Fire on Command. In command mode, an actual successful personal shot permits the unit's volley. |

Q uses the original contextual order and formation placement paths. Unit speed, fatigue, terrain, run permission, formation geometry and individual path completion stay native. Current targeting refreshes the crosshair context and filters the possessed formation out of its own target ray. Native roster queries outside FPS camera work retain their normal behavior; camera lookup resolves the exact currently owned soldier. A guarded native UI write path addresses a reproduced cause of camera-follow changes without weakening current ownership, death or routing checks.

## Move, fight and look around

Use **WASD** to move, **Shift** to run and the mouse to look. Movement follows the unit's native parameters. Personal movement is locked while knocked down and until the native get-up animation finishes; knockback, root motion and the recovery animation continue normally.

Press **1 / 2** for melee/ranged equipment. Hold **RMB** to ready a supported ranged weapon and press **LMB** to fire/release. **Tab** cycles available native ammunition. Hold **Ctrl** for crouched firing on supported, unlocked firearm sets. Native ammunition, reload, projectile speed, gravity and collision are retained. Other soldiers keep their independent weapon tasks and AI.

Shot spread forms a circular muzzle cone driven by the selected unit's native effective accuracy, including its native type, experience and modifier providers. Firearm crosshair spacing shows the cone width. Other projectile reticles use descending dashes for drop reference. The cone is not an exact reproduction of stock rectangular scatter, and the dashes are not calibrated range marks.

Melee uses native paired combat with a compact top-centre meter and a larger **LMB / RMB** mouse-button cue. Response time lasts at least three active simulation seconds and one complete indicator cycle; actual pause freezes the clock. **INTERRUPTED** explains a canceled admitted prompt. Horizontal and vertical head-look continue through QTE and native kill-animation handoff, with a 100-degree yaw limit on either side of the animated head. The native eye position, roll, actors, combat outcome and animation/root-motion timing remain intact.

First-person presentation uses a closer owned near plane and eye-relative infantry weapon/arm look, preserving native head/shoulder caches and neck/collar size. **F10 / Escape** restores the RTS camera position, direction and field of view saved before possession. Native death presentation precedes the eventual return to command.

## Cavalry, sieges and HUD

Mounted possession retains the rider/horse pair with separate looking and steering. At a supported climbable siege wall, use the **F** prompt to enter, **W / S** to ascend/descend, and release those keys to hold. Climbing follows native speed; the native crest animation and landing preserve possession continuity.

The automatic HUD shows unit/clan information, personal equipment/ammunition, accuracy/drop reticles, contextual commands and combat/traversal prompts. The native weapon catalog includes base-game, Rise of the Samurai and Fall of the Samurai records; recognition is broader than confirmed live coverage of every unit and ammunition set.

## Installation and validation

Close SHOGUN 2 completely before running the v0.19 setup, then start a fresh game normally through Steam. The integrated loader starts possession, HUD and QTE automatically. The player installer does not install the optional diagnostic recorder.

Release loader and full installer checks are freshly run, including exact Test109 and published-v0.18 upgrades, seven interrupted rollback stages for each and repeat-install backup retention. Earlier predecessor fixtures remain covered. Gameplay binaries and evidence are retained: 61 adapter and 5 HUD executables from Test109, and 36 QTE executables from Test108. These gameplay suites were not rerun for this metadata-only promotion. Test108 preview/viewport evidence and Test109 pixel-equivalence checks are retained.

The player accepted Test109 after playing without first sending READY. Automatic capture still ran. Retrospective module/HUD hashes, native presentation and recording telemetry establish the tested build; no pre-play readiness verification is claimed. Acceptance is scoped to the played build, not every ally-hit weapon, roster, map, driver or long-run lifecycle. Release-specific v0.19 loader/cache identity is separate from installed Test109. See [validation](v0.19-validation.json), the full [README](../../README.md) and [build instructions](../../BUILD_AND_TEST.md).

Native effects and audio-thread crashes remain unresolved. The latest captured crash occurred with blood effects disabled and is an invalid DirectSound virtual call, distinct from the earlier effects-table and Miles immediate faults. The corrupting write/lifetime and mod involvement are unproven. No crash fix is included. Mounted-bow presentation, specialty-unit/animation coverage, possession exits, multiplayer/overhaul compatibility and every castle lifecycle remain limitations. Naval/ship and crew possession are not implemented; stock artillery first-person behavior is retained. See [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
