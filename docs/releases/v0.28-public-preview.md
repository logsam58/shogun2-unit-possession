# Shogun 2 First-Person Mod — v0.28

Take your place in the battle.

Select one of your units, press **F9**, and fight as one of its soldiers. Your army keeps fighting around you, and **F10** puts you back in command from above. This mod brings first- and third-person play into the original **Total War: SHOGUN 2**.

## What you can do

- Fight on foot or horseback, with your soldier's own weapons and equipment.
- Choose a soldier up close, including generals, officers, flag bearers and musicians.
- Aim bows, guns and thrown weapons; hold and release bow shots to vary their strength.
- Fight through timed melee prompts and native combat animations, with charging and siege climbing.
- Switch to third person, change the distance and orbit around your soldier while moving.
- Give your unit move, attack, formation, stop, hold, follow and fire orders without returning to the RTS view.
- Use your unit's abilities and special ammunition, with native restrictions and cooldowns.

The HUD shows your condition, ammo, abilities and commands. K hides the normal HUD in both views while keeping melee prompts visible. Friendly fire also earns the familiar **“SHAMEFUL DISPLAY!”**

## Install

**Private preview:** download the installer from this draft's Assets below. The repository remains private. Game-resource redistribution must be resolved before offering it as a public download.

Choose **`Shogun2-First-Person-v0.28-Setup.exe`** for the player installation. Close the game, run setup, and select the folder containing **Shogun2.exe** and **Empire.Retail.dll**. Launch normally through Steam, enter a single-player land battle, select a friendly unit and press **F9**.

The setup uses Inno Setup and adds a Windows uninstall entry. Close the game before updates or removal. No compiler, Python installation, separate launcher, recorder or debugger is needed to play.

For developers, **`Shogun2FP-Public-Source-Kit.zip`** is the separately prepared source/local-resource kit. Follow the [build guide](https://github.com/logsam58/shogun2-first-person/blob/public-release/BUILD_AND_TEST.md) to prepare resources from an owned copy of the supported game. This existing source archive records its own source revision; it has not been regenerated for this presentation update.

## Controls at a glance

**WASD** moves, **Shift** runs, **P** switches camera views, and **RMB/LMB** ready and fire your weapon. Follow the indicated mouse prompt in melee.

**Q** moves or attacks where you're aiming; hold and drag it to lay out a formation. **E** stops, **Z** holds your position and facing, **C** calls the unit alongside you, and **R** changes fire orders. Double-tap Q or Z to run. **1** toggles native melee mode, **T** selects an ability or special ammunition, and **G** uses it.

See the [full control guide](https://github.com/logsam58/shogun2-first-person/blob/public-release/README.md#controls) for camera orbit, mounting, climbing and the remaining keys.

## Requirements and known limitations

Requires an owned, installed copy of the original Windows Steam version of Shogun 2 and the supported engine build. The standard setup requires Windows 10 or later. Intended for single-player land battles and sieges; multiplayer, overhauls and every expansion/unit combination aren't verified.

Crashes can still occur. Ultra unit size with several armies or dense fighting can cause serious slowdowns. Some first-person weapons and arms clip, mounted bows have visual issues, and bow draw audio remains tied to the native animation. Native charge encounters don't guarantee a kill or cavalry leap.

Read [known issues](https://github.com/logsam58/shogun2-first-person/blob/public-release/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md) before treating an untested combination as supported.
