# v0.28 — Shogun 2 First-Person Mod

Join the battle as one of your soldiers, fight with their own weapons, and command the unit from the front. Switch between first and third person while your army keeps fighting around you. The [README](../../README.md) has the full controls.

Download **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.28-LocalAssets-Setup.exe**. Close Shogun 2, run setup and choose your original Steam installation. It prepares the artwork, unit cards, ability icons, fonts and cropped “Shameful display!” voice from your own game. Those assets are not bundled in the download, and you do not need developer tools to play.

The installer is unsigned. Supported English game resources and Windows Media Foundation are required.

## Known bugs

- **Charging sometimes works and sometimes does not.** It remains intermittent.
- **Castle wall climbing sometimes works and sometimes does not.** It remains intermittent.
- Crashes, Ultra unit-size slowdowns and some first-person clipping remain unresolved. See the [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) for details.

**Shogun2FP-Public-Source-Kit-v0.28.zip** contains the source and build tools without game assets. Check the downloads against **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
