# v0.28 — Private local-resource preview

Join the battle as one of your soldiers, fight with their own weapons, and command the unit from the front. Switch between first and third person; your army keeps fighting around you. Full controls are in the [public-release README](../../README.md).

Download **Shogun2-First-Person-v0.28-LocalAssets-Setup.exe**. Close Shogun 2, run setup and choose your original Steam installation. Setup reads the artwork, cards, ability icons, fonts and cropped “Shameful display!” voice from your game. Those resources are not in this download. No developer tools are needed to play. Supported English game resources and Windows Media Foundation are required.

This is **Test159**, an unsigned private candidate. Native and isolated installer checks are recorded separately; no real installation or gameplay acceptance is claimed. The installed personal mod is unchanged. Unresolved crashes, Ultra crowd slowdown and presentation limits remain listed in [known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

**Shogun2FP-Public-Source-Kit-Test159.zip** contains the maintainable source/build tools, without game assets. The earlier standard setup and frozen source kit are historical Test158 private previews, not the local-resource package. Published v0.28/v0.27 assets are unchanged. This draft and repository remain private.
