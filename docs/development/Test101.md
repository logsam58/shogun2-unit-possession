# Test101: aimed native placement, stable follow and close-model presentation

Unpublished correction candidate. Test100 was rejected in live play for Q deployment, continuous Follow Me movement when looking around, and rifle/arm clipping. The published v0.15 release and exact Test100 rollback remain preserved. Live acceptance is pending.

Q keeps normal first-person mouse-look active. Press and release use the game's stock right-button messages at the client-center aim point; mouse movement keeps that point centered while the view changes. Aim at terrain, hold Q, move aim across the intended frontage and release Q. The native cursor, preview, frontage calculation and commitment remain in the engine. The native context cursor stays centered and provides the aim cue. There is no free-cursor switch, custom formation geometry or Q movement-packet builder. Cancellation has a distinct private message ID so it cannot consume the saved RTS camera-return message.

Follow Me issues its initial normal native formation even while the player is stationary. Subsequent goals require actual horizontal movement, with the existing distance/cadence bound. Formation heading comes from travel after that first order. Body yaw or mouse-look alone cannot restart the order. E/Z/R retain Test100 behavior.

The observed stock soldier camera has a 0.15 m near plane. The current living first-person view uses at most 0.02 m, preserving an already smaller native value and the native far plane. The native projection is still calculated by the game. The exact captured near distance is restored only on the matching camera and matching presented value. Native death view retains its original distance. This can change first-person depth precision; distant surfaces and effects require live observation.

Infantry ranged-look rotates the arms and weapon around the fresh current eye, converted into model space, rather than the shoulder midpoint. The same pivot feeds the muzzle correction. Native animation, ammunition, accuracy and actor/world collision remain native. Private eye samples now identify their lease/actor/unit; stale samples do not authorize this presentation. Mounted pose algorithms and native paired-combat rigs are unchanged. The neck/collar is kept full-sized: bone hierarchy alone does not establish exclusive skin weights. Raw native head and shoulder-axis cache sampling and exact palette restoration are retained.

Validation includes actual production Follow Me service with stationary changing yaw, active-Q worker look, stock mouse decoder/cursor lifecycle, native unprojection and stock drag distance, original native projection reproducing the 15 cm cuts, exact interface restoration/foreign-camera guards, released-RMB/reload behavior and view-relative arm/muzzle geometry. Window/world hosting and geometry providers are fixture substitutions. Actual game-window dispatch, native world formation rendering, full AI path completion, rifle meshes, fighting, wall interactions and roster coverage still require recorded play.

No new native hook or published numeric protocol layout is introduced. HUD and QTE source/binaries are retained; there is no fresh QTE or HUD render claim for this candidate. Automatic crash recording remains independent of READY, pause or mod availability. The known effects crash is unresolved.
