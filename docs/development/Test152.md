# Test152 — leaders, camera and routing combat

Development candidate for v0.27. Published v0.27 is unchanged. These changes require a fresh game process and user testing; offline checks do not establish rendered gameplay success.

- At the closest RTS zoom, F9 can choose a native officer or general under the actual cursor within the selected unit. Their native actor configuration and personal ranged-action/projectile record determine their own equipment. Unit-wide orders and abilities still apply to their unit.
- F first turns the body toward the validated native wall path using native turning intent. The original attachment, traversal rate, climb-over and landing remain responsible for climbing.
- During a QTE or its paired combat animation, the camera follows the current animated face. Eye look is limited to 35 degrees of yaw and 25 degrees of pitch either way. Initial native alignment remains untouched; ordinary walking mouse look remains unchanged.
- P switches first/third person while possessed. The wheel moves the third-person camera by half a metre per notch, between 1 and 12 metres. An original world-only query brings the camera forward before obstructions. Third person renders the native full model. Commands and ability targeting use the displayed camera; release still obtains the projectile origin from the native weapon.
- Broken/shattered morale no longer ejects a living possessed soldier. When a native engagement is selected, a broken/shattered player loses automatically; otherwise a broken/shattered opponent loses automatically. Both routing means the player loses. No QTE is shown. Native preparation selects and plays the authored fatal outcome; there is no new kill clip, damage write or distant-target scan. A morale break during an active prompt transitions that same pair into native playback. Normal/wavering combat and normal charge outcomes retain their existing policy.

The preserved on-demand asset decoder actually decoded twelve officer variants with independent byte roundtrips. Appearance was not used as proof of weapon capability. Seventeen focused Ghidra functions were exported read-only, and eleven additional indexed role/query functions were instruction-byte checked against the supported engine. Native351740/353d50 establish the general/officer types;391b50 establishes per-actor shot availability and projectile override. Decompiled analysis is not original source or a verified function signature.

Focused fixtures cover exact leader type/identity/configuration and cursor choice, original391b50 projectile override/fallback, native wall-turn intent before attachment at four angles, P/wheel ownership and RTS handoff, native world-query dispatch and camera pull-in, full native model presentation, current-face eye bounds, no-QTE routing selection/roles, mid-prompt Session handoff and routing-only retargeting. Geometry, allocation, window/rendering and original preparation/clip outputs are explicitly provided where full game services are unavailable. Full adapter/QTE/loader/installer and Python checks are recorded separately.

Exact Test151 rollback is preserved. No game settings, CPU affinity, recorder configuration, assets or public Status ABI are changed; there are no new native hooks. The ordinary D: recorder remains armed. No automatic launch or extra profiling is performed. Fresh READY/IN BATTLE and exact loaded hashes plus actual recording must be checked before formal testing.

Enemy acquisition of an isolated player, prior general crash reports, Ultra crowd limits and previously deferred first-person model quality remain separate open work. This candidate does not claim to solve them.
