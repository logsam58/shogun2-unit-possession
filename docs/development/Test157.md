# Test157 — soldier roles and General possession

This development build expands possession to the native human soldier roles in your selected unit: regular soldiers, Generals, officers, standard bearers and musicians. It also recognizes the native naval leader actor classes; that does not add ship movement or vehicle possession. Published v0.27 remains unchanged.

The old shared whitelist explicitly rejected standards and musicians. Several control paths also assumed that only Generals and officers could have their own movement configuration. The native constructors show that these special roles share the soldier layout and common movement/animation virtuals. Each actor keeps its own configuration, action definitions and personal projectile record. A standard bearer without a native shoot action does not inherit the formation's bow or firearm.

Two additional restrictions were found. The QTE camera reader still demanded the regular-soldier vtable in both bulk and sparse read paths, despite the combat reader supporting Generals. Foot acquisition also imposed posture<18 on special configurations, although native3e4850 maps native postures through0x5c. Both paths now use the shared role and existing root/attachment/lifetime policy. Recovery, reciprocal mount ownership, unit/serial identity, holder membership, native phase and heartbeat checks remain. The precise cause of the user's particular General rejection was not recorded and is not claimed as proved.
